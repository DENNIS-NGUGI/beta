Universal Health Coverage - Three Funds
Primary Healthcare Fund

Government funding for essential health services accessible to all registered members.

  • Outpatient services
  • Inpatient services
  • Maternity Services
  • Optical services
  • End of life services
  • Screening & management
Social Health Insurance Fund

Individual premium-based insurance for comprehensive healthcare coverage.

  • Renal care treatment
  • Oncology services
  • Mental Wellness services
  • Oral health services
  • Surgical services
  • Overseas treatment
Emergency & Critical Illness Fund

Government funding for emergency and critical healthcare needs.

  • Emergency evacuation
  • Critical care (ICU/HDU)
  • Chronic illness
  • Assistive devices
  • Palliative care
  • Stabilization services
